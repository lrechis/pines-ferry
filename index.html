How responding works
Tapping “Count me in” or “I’m interested” opens an email to the group, pre-filled with the special’s details. Add your name and number of people, then send. Posts to the group are moderated.

About this page — please read

This page is a community summary, not an operator. It is compiled by volunteers from posts to the Pines Winter Ferry mailing list. It is not run by Sayville Ferry, H2O Limo, FIPPOA, or any other boat operator, and it is not affiliated with or endorsed by any of them. Nothing here is an official schedule, booking, or guarantee from any operator.

Each trip is the organizer’s responsibility. The person named as Organizer on each run is that trip’s primary point of contact — they arrange the boat, keep the headcount, set the cost share, and announce any change or cancellation. Questions about a specific trip go to its organizer, through the mailing list. This page only reflects what was posted; if a time or headcount changes on the list, the list is the source of truth.

Follow the mailing list directly. The list is where specials are proposed, confirmed, and changed — this page can lag behind it. To watch the group’s activity, open the group online, visit its page on Google Groups, or subscribe by email so new posts come straight to your inbox.
