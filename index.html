About this page — please read

This page is a volunteer-compiled community summary of posts to the Pines Winter Ferry mailing list. It is not run by Sayville Ferry, H2O Limo, FIPPOA, or any other boat operator, and it is not affiliated with or endorsed by any of them.

Each trip is the responsibility of its named organizer, who is the primary point of contact for booking, headcount, cost share, and any change or cancellation.
